Website and staff portal proposal

A new website and candidate tracking for HCC-Temps

A premium, easy-to-use website that puts candidate registration first, plus a secure portal where candidates upload their documents and your team tracks every application from first interest to ready to work.

1Scope of work

Package A · Website refresh

£1,500
  • Premium, simple design based on the approved mockup
  • Easy navigation on phone and desktop
  • Candidate registration as the main priority
  • Key roles featured: nurses, senior care assistants and cleaning staff
  • Section for companies looking to work with you, with a request cover form
  • All active job listings moved to the new site, with search and filters
  • Apply with details and CV, sent to your staffing email
  • Your logo, photos, slogan and accreditations; copy updated throughout

Package B · Staff portal and automated candidate tracking

£2,000

For candidates

  • Invited after CV review, then set their own password
  • Secure login with a code sent by email
  • Progress bar from applied to ready to work
  • Document checklist for their role, upload by phone camera or file, with photo tips
  • See each document's status and messages from your team
  • Automatic emails whenever something changes

For your team

  • Dashboard: applications, pipeline, ready to work, average onboarding time, outstanding documents by type
  • Filter by the last 24 hours, 30, 60 or 90 days, 6 or 12 months
  • Every candidate filterable by sector, role, stage and documents
  • Candidate folder: view, download, approve or request changes, internal and shared notes, full activity log
  • Candidate report, Excel export of all records and activity
  • Staff accounts with secure logins; files stored privately and renamed automatically

Website first. The staff portal runs inside the website, so Package B starts once Package A is complete.
