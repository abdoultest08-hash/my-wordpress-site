2 · How applications work

Automatic from apply to upload

Everything runs inside your own WordPress site. There are no extra subscriptions, and candidate data stays on your server.

  1. Candidate applies with their name, phone, email and CV. They get a reference number such as HCC-001234.
  2. Your team gets an email tagged [JOB APPLICATION] with the candidate's details and the CV attached.
  3. The candidate gets an email with a private upload link and the list of documents their role needs. The link expires after 14 days, and your team can send a new one with one click.
  4. They upload documents from their phone, in one go or over several visits. Each file is renamed, for example HCC-001234_Shah-Priya_DBS_2026-10-20.pdf, and stored privately.
  5. Your team is told by email, and the applicant's record shows what is in and what is missing.

ID, DBS and right-to-work documents are never attached to emails and are never public. Only logged-in staff can download them.
