Start hereHow the system fits together

There are two places to work. Most day-to-day work happens in the Staff portal. The WordPress dashboard is for the website itself: jobs, settings and user accounts.

WhereWhat you do thereAddress
Staff portalReview candidates and documents, send updates, record checks and references, sign candidates off, download files, add staff.hcctemps.co.uk/staff
WordPress dashboardJob adverts, importing jobs, deleting candidate records, website settings, About us team, user accounts.hcctemps.co.uk/wp-admin
Candidate portalWhat candidates see: their answers, documents, training and your updates. You can open it read-only with View as candidate.hcctemps.co.uk/my-application

The life of a registration

  1. The candidate creates an account and confirms their email with a 6-digit code.
  2. They answer the pre-screening questions, then upload documents and training certificates. Anything they don't have yet can be marked "I don't have this yet".
  3. When they submit, the staffing inbox gets a full summary with their CV attached.
  4. You review in the Staff portal: approve, ask for replacements, request missing items, record checks and references.
  5. Your changes collect in an Update tray and go to the candidate in one email when you press Send.
  6. When everything is complete you press Finish onboarding. The candidate moves to Staff completed and gets a "ready to work" email.

Staff portalLogging in

On the website, choose Log in → Staff login, or go straight to hcctemps.co.uk/candidate-login/?for=staff.

  1. Make sure I'm HCC staff is selected at the top.
  2. Type your work email.
  3. Type your password.
  4. Tick I'm not a robot. Sometimes a short sum appears here instead ("Prove your humanity"): type the answer.
  5. Press Log in.
  1. Type the 6-digit code from the email "Your HCC-Temps login code". It works for 15 minutes.
  2. Press Verify (it often logs you in on its own once the 6 digits are typed).
  3. No email? Check spam, then press Resend Code. Only the newest code works.
TipIf you clicked a link in an email (for example "Open in Staff portal" for one candidate), you'll land on that candidate after logging in.

Staff portalThe dashboard

The first page after logging in. Go back to it any time with Dashboard in the dark blue bar.

  1. Figures at a glance: candidates in progress, candidates with files to review, candidates with updates not sent, completed in the last 30 days, and the average days from registering to sign-off.
  2. Updates not sent: candidates where someone made changes (approvals, replace requests, notes) but hasn't pressed Send yet. It shows who made the changes and how long they've waited. Press Open and send. Nothing is ever emailed to a candidate automatically.
  3. Tabs: In progress, Staff completed, and Withdrawn / not qualified. Click a candidate's row to open their folder.
  4. Search by name, reference, role, email or phone, then press Enter.
  5. Export to Excel (CSV) downloads the list in the current tab.
Tags on a row"possible duplicate" means another record has the same NI number or phone number. "update not sent" means the tray has unsent changes.

Staff portalA candidate's folder

Everything about one candidate is on one page: documents and training on the left, then answers, the update tray, checks, references, declaration, bank details, notes and the activity log on the right. Use Expand all / Collapse all to open or fold every section.

  1. View as candidate: opens their portal read-only in a new tab, exactly as they see it.
  2. Approve all uploaded: approves every document that is uploaded and waiting for review.
  3. Complete profile (100%): approves what's uploaded, then asks you to override each remaining item one at a time, with a reason and a note (see Mark complete).
  4. Download all (zip): every file in checklist order, plus a summary page.
  5. Candidate report: everything on one printable page.
  6. Stage: a label for where the candidate is (New registration, Documents, Checks in progress, Ready to work). Changing it saves straight away.
  7. Withdrawn / not qualified: closes the application (see Finishing or closing).
Possible duplicateIf a pink box says "Possible duplicate", open the other record to check. If it's a different person, press Checked: not a duplicate.

Staff portalReviewing documents

  1. Tick boxes: tick several items to act on them together.
  2. With ticked items: Approve (only items that have a file), Request as missing, or Mark complete (override).
  3. View opens the file in a new tab. Download saves it. Both are recorded in the activity log.
  4. Status: To review, Approved, Replace requested, Requested, Missing, Completed by staff, Not needed.
  5. ✓ approves this one document.
  6. ⋯ opens more actions for this document.
  1. Choose why it needs replacing (blurry, corners cut off, expired, name doesn't match, wrong document, both sides needed).
  2. Ask candidate to replace adds the request to the update for the candidate.
  3. Type a note about this document.
  4. Add to update for candidate: the note goes to the candidate in the next update email, and shows on that document in their portal.
  5. Save as staff-only note: never shown to the candidate.
When a candidate replaces a fileThe document goes back to "To review", the old file is kept (the new one is named "(v2)"), and the staffing inbox gets one "[DOCUMENTS]" email listing everything they uploaded in that visit.

Staff portalThe update email to the candidate

Approvals, replace requests, missing items and notes for the candidate all collect in the Update for [name] card at the top right. They go in one email when you press Send. Nothing is sent automatically.

  1. The number of changes waiting.
  2. The changes, grouped. Press remove next to one to take it out of this email (the approval itself stays).
  3. Preview and send email shows exactly what the candidate will receive.
  4. Press Send email. The update also appears in the candidate's portal and in the Sent to candidate card.
Don't forget to sendUnsent changes show on the dashboard under Updates not sent until someone presses Send. You can't finish onboarding while changes are unsent.

Staff portalMark complete (override)

Use this when an item is satisfied without an upload, for example you saw the original passport in the office, or a document isn't needed for this candidate. You'll be asked about each item in turn.

  1. Choose the reason.
  2. Write a note. This is required, for example "Original passport checked in the Leeds office, copy on file".
  3. Confirm and next moves to the next item.
  4. Skip this item leaves it as it is.

Your name, the reason and the note are saved in the activity log. Optional items ("where applicable") that weren't provided aren't counted, so you won't be asked about them.

Staff portalChecks, references and declaration

Checks

  1. Open a check, then choose the Result: Pass, Fail or Follow-up needed.
  2. Fill in the visa expiry date (right to work, non-British candidates) and the outcome or notes.
  3. Open official check opens the government or NMC website in a new tab.
  4. Press Save check. The date and your name are filled in for you.

References

  1. The date you sent the reference request.
  2. The date it came back.
  3. The outcome (Satisfactory, Concerns, Not received), then Save.
  4. Replace referee if one doesn't reply (the old one is kept in the history), or + Add another referee.

Declaration

  1. Tick Declaration sent to candidate when you send it.
  2. Tick Signed declaration received when it comes back, then press Save.

Staff portalNotes and the activity log

  1. Choose Internal compliance note (staff only) or Message to candidate (goes in the next update email).
  2. Write the note.
  3. For internal notes you can add who signed it off, the next action and when it's due. These are optional.
  4. Press Save to profile.

Everything is recorded with the date, time and who did it: uploads, replacements, logins, approvals, notes, checks, emails sent, views and downloads of files, and email changes.

Staff portalChanging a candidate's email

If a candidate typed their email wrongly or has a new one, they can fix it themselves:

  • While registering: on the "Check your email" step, Wrong email address? Change it.
  • After registering: in their portal, under My answers, Change my email address. A code is sent to the new address.

If they phone you instead, change it from their folder:

  1. Open Change email address.
  2. Type the new email.
  3. Press Change email. It becomes their login straight away. The old and new addresses both get a short notice, and it's written in the activity log.

Staff portalFinishing onboarding, or closing

  1. Each condition shows ✓ or ✗: profile 100%, declaration sent, signed declaration received, no unsent updates.
  2. The name of the person signing off.
  3. When everything is ✓, press Finish onboarding and move to Staff completed. The candidate gets a "ready to work" email.
Made a mistake?On a completed candidate, press Move back to in progress. The sign-off is removed and the change is logged.

Withdrawn / not qualified

  1. Choose the outcome: Withdrawn by candidate, Not qualified, or Not suitable.
  2. Write the reason.
  3. Your name (signed off by) and the date.
  4. Press Close application. The candidate moves to the Withdrawn / not qualified tab and their portal becomes read-only. Reopen application brings them back.

Staff portalViewing and downloading files

  • One file: in the Documents list, View opens it in a new tab and Download saves it.
  • Everything: Download all (zip) at the top of the folder. You get a folder named "HCC-001072 Alex Morgan - Registered Nurse" with documents numbered in checklist order (01 - Alex Morgan - CV.pdf…), a Training subfolder, and a summary page.
  • Printable summary: Candidate report.
Where are the files kept?Candidate documents are stored in a private folder on the website. They are not in the WordPress Media Library, and there's no public link to them. Always open them through the Staff portal: every view and download is logged. Managers see bank details in full in reports and zips; staff see the last 4 digits.
  1. The yellow bar shows it's a read-only staff preview. Back to their folder returns you.
  2. The candidate's quick-jump bar: Updates, My answers, Documents, Training.

Staff portalAdding staff to the Staff portal

Managers can add staff without going into WordPress.

  1. Open Team in the dark blue bar (managers only).
  2. First name (and last name).
  3. Their work email.
  4. Staff or Manager (managers see bank details in full and can add staff).
  5. Press Add and email invite. They get an email with a link to set their password, then log in at Staff login with an emailed code.

To remove someone's access, see Admin access and login codes.

WordPress dashboardJob Board: add, change, close

  1. Job Board in the left menu.
  2. Add new job to create one.
  3. Click a job's title to change it.
  4. Import jobs (CSV) to add or update many jobs from a spreadsheet (next page).
  1. The job title, as shown on the site.
  2. The description: what they'll do, what they need, what they get.
  3. Tick the Sector on the right, and below it the Job type, Location and Contract. Use "+ Add" to create a new one, for example a new town.
  4. Press Publish (new job) or Update (existing job).
  1. Pay as shown on the site, e.g. "£14.20 – £15.00 per hour".
  2. The lowest hourly rate as a number, e.g. 14.20. Used for "sort by pay" and Google Jobs.
  3. Shift pattern, e.g. "Long days, nights".
  4. Badge: New or Urgent.
  5. Closing date: the job disappears from the site by itself after this date.

To close a job

Either set a closing date in the past, or in Job status (right side) tick Expired Jobs instead of Live Jobs and press Update. Closed jobs stay in the dashboard so you can reopen them later. Move to Trash removes a job completely.

WordPress dashboardImporting jobs from a spreadsheet

  1. Download the CSV template and open it in Excel or Google Sheets. Keep the first row (column names); one job per row.
  2. Save as CSV UTF-8, then choose the file.
  3. Press Upload and preview. The preview shows each row as New, Update or Skip (with the reason). Press Import to finish.
Updating, not duplicatingA row with the same ref (or the same web address in the slug column) as an existing job updates that job. To close jobs, put expired in the status column. Several locations go in one cell separated by |, e.g. "Leeds | Bradford".

WordPress dashboardDeleting a candidate

For test registrations, duplicates, or someone who needs to start again (for example "There is already an account with this email" and they can't get in).

  1. Open Candidates in the left menu.
  2. Hover over the candidate. Open in Staff portal takes you to their folder.
  3. Press Trash.
  1. Click Trash at the top of the list.
  2. Restore brings a record back if it was a mistake.
  3. Delete Permanently removes the record, all their uploaded files and their login. The email can then be used to register again.
  4. Empty Trash permanently deletes everything in the trash.
Trash alone is not enoughA candidate in the Trash still has a login, so their email still says "already registered". Permanent deletion can't be undone: download the zip first if you need to keep anything.

WordPress dashboardAdmin access and login codes

  1. Users → Add New User.
  2. Username and email (use their work email for both).
  3. Tick Send the new user an email about their account so they can set a password.
  4. Choose the Role: Administrator (everything), HCC Manager or HCC Staff (Staff portal only).
  5. Press Add New User.

Login codes (two-step verification)

  • HCC Staff, HCC Managers and candidates always get a 6-digit code by email at login. This needs the free Two Factor plugin to be active (Plugins menu). If it's switched off, a red notice appears in the dashboard.
  • Administrators can turn the same code on for themselves: Users → Profile → Two-Factor Options → Email.

Removing someone's access

Users → All Users, hover over the person, then Delete (WordPress asks what to do with any content they wrote; choose to give it to another administrator). To change someone's level, edit the user and change Role.

ReferenceTroubleshooting

A candidate typed the wrong email when registering

On the "Check your email" step they press Wrong email address? Change it and a new code goes to the right address. If they've closed the page, change it for them in their folder (Changing a candidate's email), then they log in with the new email.

A candidate says the code didn't arrive, or "That code isn't right"

  • Check spam or junk.
  • Use send a new code. Wait 30 seconds between requests, and only the newest code works.
  • After 5 wrong tries the code locks; sending a new code unlocks it.
  • The activity log in their folder shows each code sent and why one was refused.

"There is already an account with this email"

They already registered. They should use Log in, or Forgotten your password? (both links appear under the message). If they need to start again from scratch, delete the old record permanently (Deleting a candidate).

Someone forgot their password

On the login page, Forgotten your password?, then enter their email. They get a reset link. This works for candidates and staff.

A staff member isn't getting the login code

Check spam. Check the Two Factor plugin is active (Plugins). Check their email in Users is right. They can press Resend Code.

"Too many attempts. Please wait a while or call us."

The website limits repeated form attempts from one internet connection to stop spam. It clears by itself within an hour.

An upload fails

Files must be PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC or Word, up to 10MB. Phone photos are made smaller automatically. If a candidate has been away a long time, ask them to log in again.

A candidate's portal or a page looks out of date

Ask them to refresh the page. After a website update, clear the WordPress.com cache once (WordPress.com dashboard → Settings → Caching → Clear all caches).

Emails aren't arriving in the staffing inbox

Check Staffing inbox in Appearance → Customize → HCC-Temps → Contact details and emails, and check spam. If emails go to spam, the domain's email settings (SPF and DKIM) may need to be set up by whoever manages the domain.

I approved or finished something by mistake

  • An approval: use ⋯ → Ask candidate to replace, or remove the line from the update tray before sending.
  • Finish onboarding: Move back to in progress.
  • A closed application: Reopen application.

Everything you do is in the activity log.

"Possible duplicate"

Another record has the same NI number or phone. Open it to check. If it's the same person, close or delete the extra one. If not, press Checked: not a duplicate.
