Who We Are

Care Built On Values, Not Just Compliance

Winserve Care Services Ltd was founded on a simple belief: that the social care crisis starts with how we treat our carers. We invest in our team — because carers who feel valued give the kind of care that families remember.

Operating across Leeds and Cornwall, we provide CQC-regulated home care and supported living for people with complex needs — with 18 specialist areas and a team of 70+ dedicated professionals.

  • ✓CQC registered & rated Good — independently inspected
  • ✓18 specialist care areas with dedicated trained staff
  • ✓Serving Leeds & Cornwall with locally based teams
  • ✓Trusted by local authorities and NHS commissioners
