# West Flows: post copy style (YouTube Shorts + Instagram)

Taken from posts that have already gone out. Every new video gets copy in this style,
saved in `library/<slug>/COPY.md` as "Video NN copy".

## Shape

1. **Title line.** Plain statement of the topic, often "X - Y" with a spaced hyphen, sometimes
   one emoji at the end. Reads like a search phrase. Under ~70 characters.
2. **Blunt opener.** One or two short sentences that say the point straight. Casual, slightly
   punchy ("your whole deal calculation is screwed up").
3. **Explain it plainly.** One or two sentences, no jargon left unexplained.
4. **Arrow list** (`→`) only when there are real steps or numbers. Use the actual figures from the video.
5. **The catch or takeaway**, if there is one ("It's a patch, not a permanent fix").
6. **CTA in first person**, tied to the topic:
   - numbers / mortgages / deals / business → "my calculator" ("My calculator does it properly.",
     "Run your own numbers before you commit to a deal - my calculator does the heavy lifting.")
   - landlords / accommodation / providers → "my landlord page"
7. `Link in bio 👆` on the last line (optional when there's no CTA).

## Rules

- British English, £, real numbers from the video only (no details from other videos).
- Sounds like he talks: "you", "I", short sentences, no corporate phrasing, no hashtags.
- No em dashes. The spaced hyphen " - " is fine (he uses it).
- 60-130 words. Shorter is better.

Renting out a house on a residential mortgage - here's the legal way 📋

It's not automatically illegal, it becomes a problem once you get caught.

If your plan changes after buying, the process is called consent to let:

→ Live in the property for 6-12 months first
→ Go back to your lender with a genuine reason (relocation, etc.)
→ Ask for a letter confirming they're happy for you to rent it out
→ That letter usually covers 12-24 months

It's a patch, not a permanent fix. Once it expires you either renew the story or switch to a proper buy-to-let mortgage.
