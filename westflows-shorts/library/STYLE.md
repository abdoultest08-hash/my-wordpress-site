# West Flows Shorts: house style

Written from the 7 shorts in `reference/` (1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264 ~8-11 Mbps).
Contact sheets: `library/reference/*.jpg`.

| Area | Reference | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 31-110s, most 65-80s | Aim 30-75s. Up to 90s if the story holds. Instagram takes it; YouTube Shorts allow up to 3 min |
| Framing | Waist-up to full upper body, on site in the property. One short uses a split screen (wide on top, close-up below) | 9:16 crop from the 4K frame following the face. Punch-ins up to 1.3x for emphasis |
| Captions | Montserrat ExtraBold, ALL CAPS, white, soft drop shadow, no outline. ~56px, 2-4 words a page, max 2 lines, centred at ~1480-1600px from the top | Same. `brand.ts` matches it |
| Highlight | 1-2 **key** words in amber orange `#F6A404` (numbers, "commercial", "find out"), not word-by-word karaoke | `highlight` list per short in `props.json` |
| Hook | None. Shorts start mid-sentence ("During the week...", "At the end of...") | **Change:** open on the strongest line (curiosity, a number, a bold claim) plus a short on-screen hook title for ~2s |
| Pace | Hard cuts on the same framing; pauses mostly trimmed; walk-and-talk footage | Cut pauses, fillers, stutters and retakes so it flows |
| B-roll | Cutaways to the ceiling, walls and windows from the same shoot | Use shots of the property from the long video. Motion graphics (stats, ticklists, labels) for numbers |
| Music | No obvious music bed (to confirm by ear) | None by default |
| Ending | No end card; ends on the last line, sometimes abruptly | End on a complete thought. CTA in the caption text; end card optional (`endCard`) |
| Colour | Natural, slightly dark/moody inside; one short has a vignette | Natural, no grade |

## Hook formula (first 1-3 seconds)

Pick the line that makes someone stop scrolling, and move it to the start if it's later in the moment:
- **Curiosity:** "Nobody tells you this about commercial property..."
- **Number:** "They wanted £650k. We offered..."
- **Contrarian:** "Interest-only isn't the trap people think it is."
- **Stakes / mistake:** "This one mistake nearly cost us the whole deal."

On-screen hook title: max ~7 words, says the payoff or the tension, not a copy of the first line.

## Rules

- British English, £, no em dashes.
- CTA: numbers / finance / deals → deal calculator; SA / landlord → landlord page. "Link in bio".
- Keep text out of the top ~220px, bottom ~320px and right ~140px (Instagram / TikTok UI).
