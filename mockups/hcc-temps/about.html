About HCC-Temps

The right people, on shift, when you need them

Who we are

A healthcare and hospitality recruitment agency

HCC-Temps has supplied staff to care homes, hospitals, hotels and schools since 2006. We are based in London and work with clients across England.

We place registered nurses and healthcare assistants alongside chefs, kitchen staff and cleaners. Every candidate is vetted before their first shift, so you get people who are ready for the job on day one.

Our vision

Raising the standard of temporary staffing

We want to be the UK's most trusted staffing partner in healthcare and hospitality. That means sending trained, caring and reliable people who help our clients run safe, well-run services.

We treat our temps fairly, pay them properly and support their careers, because people who are looked after do better work.
