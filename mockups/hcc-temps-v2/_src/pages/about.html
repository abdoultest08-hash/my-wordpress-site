Our story

We started HCC-Temps to help

HCC-Temps was created to support the care industry: to help care homes, hospitals and hospitality businesses find reliable people, and to look after the staff who do that work.

We believe in continuity of care. Wherever we can, we send familiar, fully vetted staff so residents and guests see the same faces. Our line is open 24 hours, because care doesn't stop at 5pm.
