How quickly can you send staff?

For emergencies we cover shifts within 2 hours, anywhere in the UK. Call 0330 321 3124, 24 hours a day.

What checks do your staff have?

Right to work, photo ID, enhanced DBS where needed, two references and role-specific training. Nurses' NMC PINs are checked before every placement.

Can we have the same staff each week?

Yes. Ask for named temps and we will keep them with you wherever we can.

Do you offer temp to perm?

Yes. If you'd like to keep someone permanently, talk to us about the options.
