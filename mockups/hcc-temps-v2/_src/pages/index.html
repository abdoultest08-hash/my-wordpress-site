Frequently asked questions

Draft answers for HCC to confirm.

How do I register?

Press Register and create your account with your name, email and a password. Confirm your email, choose your role and upload your documents in your portal. You don't need a CV to start.

What documents will I need?

It depends on the role. Most people need photo ID, proof of right to work and proof of address. Care roles also need a DBS and training certificates, and nurses need their NMC PIN. Each role on the candidates page shows exactly what you need before you start.

I don't have a DBS. Can I still apply?

Yes. Register as normal and choose “No” when we ask about your DBS. We will apply for an enhanced DBS for you.

How quickly can I start work?

Most candidates are ready to work within 3 days of registering, once all documents are uploaded and checked.

Can I choose my own shifts?

Yes. Tell us when you're available and we offer you shifts that fit. You can say no to any shift.

How and when do I get paid?

Draft: you are paid weekly for the hours on your approved timesheets. HCC to confirm pay day and method.

How quickly can you send staff?

For emergencies we cover shifts within 2 hours, anywhere in the UK. Our line is open 24 hours: call 0330 321 3124.

Which roles can you cover?

Registered nurses, healthcare assistants, senior carers, chefs, kitchen assistants, catering and hospitality staff, housekeepers, cleaners and reception staff.

Are your staff vetted?

Yes. We check right to work, ID, DBS, references and role-specific training before anyone is placed with you.

Where do you supply staff?

Everywhere in the UK. Our offices are in Leeds and London and we cover nationwide.

Can I book the same temp each week?

Yes. Many clients ask for the same people so residents and guests see familiar faces. We do our best to keep them with you.

How much does it cost?

Draft: rates depend on the role, location and shift times. Request cover and we send you a quote. HCC to confirm wording.
