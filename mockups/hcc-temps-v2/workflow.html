How registration works, end to end

Every step a candidate takes, what the staffing team sees, and every automatic email. For HCC to review before we build it.

Try it as a candidateOpen the Staff portal view
The flow

Seven steps from website to ready to work

  1. 1

    Choose a role and start

    On For candidates or a job advert. The site remembers the role.

    No email
  2. 2

    Create account

    Name, surname, email, mobile, address, password, terms, privacy, robot check. Reference number created, for example HCC-001234.

    Candidate: login code
  3. 3

    Verify email

    6-digit code from the email. The same code is used at every login (2-step verification).

    Candidate: welcome
  4. 4

    Confirm role

    "You chose Registered Nurse, is this right?" Switch if it was a mistake. The checklist follows the role.

    No email
  5. 5

    Pre-screening (no uploads)

    Personal details, right to work and visa, licence and vehicle, DBS, education and experience, uniform, 2 referees, emergency contact, bank details. Submit saves everything.

    No email
  6. 6

    Documents, then training

    Upload per role. Anything they don't have: "I don't have this yet". Never blocks them. Files renamed with reference, name, type and date.

    Candidate: registration received · Staff: full summary with CV attached
  7. 7

    Portal and review

    Candidate sees details, files, statuses and messages, and can replace files. Staff approve, ask to replace, request missing items and add notes. Each change goes into an "Update for candidate" tray and is sent as one email. Overrides need a note for each item. The declaration is sent manually once everything is approved. Staff log in with an email code and robot check every time.

    Candidate: one combined update email · Staff: email on every new upload

Reminders: if a candidate stops part-way, they get a reminder after 24 hours and again after 3 days. When they log in again they carry on where they left off.

Automatic emails

Who gets what, and when

All emails are branded, sent from the website, and logged on the candidate's timeline.

Only the CV is attached. Why not the other files: passport, DBS and bank documents would otherwise sit in inboxes indefinitely and be forwarded or downloaded to phones, which is a GDPR risk. A full registration can also be 25+ files, larger than most email systems accept. The email lists every file, and staff open them securely in the Staff portal, where the latest version always is.
