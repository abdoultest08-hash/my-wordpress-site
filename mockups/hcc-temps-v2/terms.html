4. Registering as a candidate

  • We never charge work-seekers a fee for finding you work, in line with the Employment Agencies Act 1973 and the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations 2003.
  • Registering does not guarantee that we will offer you work. Offers depend on your suitability, completed checks and our clients' needs.
  • Before we can place you, we must complete checks including identity, right to work, DBS, references, professional registration and training. You can start registration without every document, but we cannot offer you shifts until the required checks are complete.
  • You can choose which shifts to accept. When you accept a shift, please attend on time or tell us as early as possible if you cannot.
  • You can ask us to close your account at any time. Some records must be kept by law, as explained in our privacy policy.

5. Documents and information you provide

  • Everything you give us must be true, complete and your own. Documents must be genuine, in date where required, and show your full name.
  • Upload clear photos or PDFs. We may ask you to upload a document again or show us the original.
  • Providing false or misleading information or documents may lead to your account being closed. Where the law requires, we may report it to the relevant authorities, such as the Home Office, the DBS or the NMC.
  • Tell us promptly if anything changes, for example your right to work, your DBS status, a professional registration matter, or a certificate that expires.
