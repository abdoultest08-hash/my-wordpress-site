1. Who we are

This website is run by HCC-Temp Staffing Ltd ("HCC-Temps", "we", "us"), a recruitment agency supplying temporary staff to the healthcare and hospitality sectors.

  • Registered in England and Wales, company number 11486985
  • Registered office: Crown House, 94 Armley Road, Armley, Leeds LS12 2EJ
  • Registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [ICO number]
  • Email: hello@hcctemps.co.uk · Phone: 0330 321 3124

We are the data controller for the personal information described in this policy. This means we decide how and why it is used, and we are responsible for keeping it safe under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Summary

  • We collect information so we can find you work, check you are safe and legally able to work, pay you, and supply staff to our clients.
  • We only share it with people who need it, such as clients you are placed with, vetting services and our payroll provider.
  • We never sell your information.
  • Your documents are stored privately and only our compliance team can see them.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

3. What we collect

If you register as a candidate

  • Identity and contact details: title, name, date of birth, address, email, phone number, a professional photograph.
  • Account details: your login email, password (stored encrypted, we cannot see it) and login records, including the one-time codes we email you.
  • Right to work: passport or driving licence, nationality, visa type, share code and supporting documents such as a certificate of sponsorship or university term-time letter.
  • Proof of address and National Insurance number.
  • Work history and qualifications: CV, education, experience, training certificates, professional registration (for example your NMC PIN and Statement of Entry), nursing diploma or degree.
  • References: the names and contact details of your referees, and the references they give.
  • Checks: your DBS certificate or Update Service details and the results of checks (see section 5).
  • Health information: immunisation records where a role requires them, and any information you choose to give us about adjustments you need.
  • Practical details: availability, preferred locations, whether you drive or have a vehicle, uniform size.
  • Payment details: bank account details and whether you work through a limited company.
  • Emergency contact: the name, relationship and contact details of your next of kin. Please tell them you have shared their details with us.
  • Work records: shifts, timesheets, pay, feedback from clients, and notes from our team.

If you are a client or request cover

  • Your name, job title, organisation, work email and phone number, site address, the shifts you need, and records of our contact and invoices.

If you visit our website or contact us

  • Messages you send through our forms, by email or by phone.
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, used to keep the site secure and working (see section 11).

Where we get it from

Mostly from you. We also receive information from your referees, the Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) and DBS umbrella bodies, the Nursing and Midwifery Council (NMC) register, the Home Office right to work service, job boards where you have posted your CV, and clients you have worked for.

5. Sensitive information

Some information is given extra protection by law: health information, information about criminal convictions (including DBS results), and information about ethnicity, religion or disability.

  • We use DBS and criminal record information only to decide whether you can safely work in roles involving children or vulnerable adults, as allowed by the Data Protection Act 2018 (Schedule 1) and the Rehabilitation of Offenders Act 1974 (Exceptions) Order 1975. We follow the DBS Code of Practice and keep this information only as long as needed.
  • We use health information, such as immunisation records, where a client or role requires it to protect patients, and to make reasonable adjustments for you. Our legal basis is our obligations in employment and social security law, and the provision of health and social care.
  • If we ask for equal opportunities information, it is optional, kept separate and used only to monitor fairness.

6. Who we share it with

We only share what is needed, with:

  • Clients you are put forward to or work for, such as care homes, hospitals, hotels and schools. We share your profile, relevant checks and compliance documents they are required to see.
  • Vetting and verification services: the DBS and DBS umbrella bodies, the NMC register, the Home Office right to work service, and identity verification providers [names to confirm].
  • Your referees, to request references.
  • Payroll, pension and accountancy providers, and HMRC.
  • IT and service providers who host our website, email and systems, such as [hosting provider, email provider and backup storage]. They act on our instructions and must keep your data secure.
  • Regulators, police and public authorities when the law requires it, for example the NMC, the Care Quality Commission, or to prevent fraud or harm.
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, auditors and insurers.
  • A buyer of our business, if HCC-Temps is ever sold or merged. They would have to protect your information in the same way.

7. Transfers outside the UK

We aim to keep your information in the UK. Some of our service providers may store data in the European Economic Area or elsewhere. When this happens, we make sure it is protected by UK adequacy regulations or by approved contract clauses, as required by UK GDPR.

When we no longer need your information, we delete it securely or make it anonymous.

9. How we protect it

  • Documents you upload are stored in a private area of our website that cannot be reached from the internet, and are only shown to logged-in members of our team.
  • Every login needs your password and a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Staff access is limited to what each person needs, and downloads are logged.
  • Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and we keep regular backups.
  • If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell you and the ICO as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your information and get a copy of it.
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete. You can update many details yourself in your candidate portal.
  • Delete your information where we no longer have a reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we use it while a concern is looked into.
  • Object to us using it on the basis of legitimate interests, or for marketing.
  • Data portability: receive information you gave us in a common format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.

To use any of these rights, email hello@hcctemps.co.uk. We will reply within one month. There is usually no charge. Some information must be kept by law even if you ask us to delete it, and we will explain if this applies.

11. Cookies

Cookies are small files saved on your device. We use:

  • Essential cookies to keep you logged in, remember your login choice ("Keep me logged in", up to 14 days), and protect forms from spam and robots (Cloudflare Turnstile). These are needed for the site to work, so we do not ask for consent.
  • Analytics cookies [if used, for example Google Analytics or Jetpack Stats] to understand how visitors use the site. We only set these if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time.

Embedded content from other sites, such as maps or videos, may set their own cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

12. Children

Our services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, we will tell registered candidates and clients by email.

14. Contact us and complaints
