## 1. Who I Am & What I Do

I run a web design and lead generation service targeting **local trade and home service businesses** in the UK and US. My offer is:

- **Option A:** Build them a professional website (one-off or monthly retainer)
- **Option B:** Generate leads for them via paid ads + their site, and charge per lead or a monthly fee

I find clients through cold email outreach — I build a personalised mock site for each prospect, screenshot it, and email them showing what their site *could* look like. My pipeline is fully automated.

**The YouTube goal:** Attract inbound leads — business owners who find my content while searching "how to get more customers as a plumber" or similar, watch my videos, trust me, and reach out to buy a website or leads package from me.

---

## 2. My Target Audience (ICP)

**Who they are:**
- Owners of small local service businesses: plumbers, roofers, electricians, cleaners/maid services, landscapers, HVAC engineers, handymen/contractors, painters, pest control, floor fitters, car detailers
- Typically 1–10 employees, owner-operated
- Age 30–55
- Technically not very confident — use phones more than laptops
- Already busy doing the work; marketing is an afterthought

**Their core problem:**
Work is feast or famine — either slammed with jobs or worried about where the next one comes from. They rely on word of mouth and want something more reliable.

**What they want:**
More calls. More booked jobs. Less slow months. They don't really want "a website" — they want **customers**.

**Their objections:**
- "I already have a Facebook page, isn't that enough?"
- "I tried Google Ads and wasted money"
- "My mate built me a website years ago"
- "I don't have time to deal with this"
- "How do I know it'll actually work?"

## 3. My Unique Angle on YouTube

I am NOT a generic marketing guru. My angle is:

> **"I'm the guy who actually builds sites and runs ads for tradespeople — here's what's actually working right now, shown with real examples."**

- I show real before/after websites (with permission or mocked)
- I share real results: "this roofer got 14 calls in 30 days from one landing page"
- I keep it dead simple — no jargon, no theory, just actionable steps
- I speak to the tradesperson, not the marketer

This beats 99% of YouTube marketing content because it's **niche-specific and practitioner-led**, not generic.

## 6. Video Format Guide

### Format A — Tutorial/Walkthrough (Best for Search)
**Length:** 8–14 minutes
**Structure:**
1. Hook (0–30s): State the exact problem. "You're not showing up on Google and it's costing you jobs."
2. Credibility (30s–1m): Who you are, why listen to you. Keep short.
3. Payoff promise (1m): "By the end of this video you'll know exactly how to fix this."
4. Main content: Step-by-step, screen recording or talking head
5. Recap (last 60s): Summarise the 3 key takeaways
6. CTA: "If you want me to do this for you, link in the description"

---

### Format B — Reaction/Teardown (Best for Engagement)
**Length:** 6–10 minutes
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "I'm looking at real plumber websites and most of them are losing customers without knowing it"
2. Screen-share review of 3–5 real (anonymised) websites
3. What's wrong + what to do instead
4. Show your work as contrast
5. CTA

---

### Format C — Case Study/Results (Best for Conversions)
**Length:** 5–8 minutes
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "This roofer was getting zero calls from his website. 30 days later: 19 enquiries."
2. The problem they had
3. What we changed (website, ads, reviews)
4. The results, shown concretely
5. CTA: "Want the same for your business?"

---

### Format D — Opinion/Myth Bust (Best for Shares)
**Length:** 4–7 minutes
**Structure:**
1. Bold statement hook: "Having a Facebook page is NOT the same as having a website. Here's why."
2. Common belief explained
3. Why it's wrong, with evidence
4. What to do instead
5. CTA

## 8. YouTube SEO Rules (Apply to Every Video)

**Title:**
- Lead with the keyword/problem
- Under 60 characters
- Use brackets for content type: [2025], [Full Guide], [Step-by-Step]
- Bad: "My top tips for plumber websites"
- Good: "Plumber Website Must-Haves to Get More Calls [2025 Checklist]"

**Description (first 150 characters matter most):**
- Start with the keyword and a clear benefit statement
- Include a CTA and link to your service in the first 3 lines

**Tags:** 8–12 tags. Mix specific ("plumber website tips") and broad ("local business marketing")

**Thumbnail:**
- Face in thumbnail performs better than text-only
- Big bold text: 3 words max, readable on phone
- High contrast colours
- Emotion: curiosity or surprise works best

**Chapter markers:** Add timestamps for every section. Boosts watch time and search visibility.

## 9. CTA Strategy (How Videos Convert to Leads)

Every video should have **one** primary CTA. Rotate between:

**CTA Option A — Free audit:**
> "Want me to take a look at your current website and tell you exactly what's stopping you from getting calls? I offer a free 10-minute audit — link in the description."

**CTA Option B — Direct offer:**
> "If you want a website like the ones I showed today, built for your trade business, I handle everything. Prices start from [X]. Link below."

**CTA Option C — Lead magnet:**
> "I've put together a free checklist of everything your website needs to get calls — download it free, link below." *(Use to build email list)*

**Description template:**

## 11. Metrics to Track (Weekly)

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Is the title + thumbnail working? |
| Average view duration | Is the content holding attention? |
| Subscriber gain per video | Is this topic attracting the right audience? |
| Comments mentioning their trade | Are tradespeople watching? |
| Description link clicks | Is the CTA converting? |
| Inbound enquiries mentioning YouTube | Ultimate success metric |

**Green flags:** CTR above 4%, average view duration above 40%, comments asking trade-specific questions.
