Who We Are

Care Built On Values, Not Just Compliance

We believe the social care crisis starts with how we treat our people. At Winserve, we invest in our carers — because staff who feel valued deliver the kind of care that families remember.

  • CQC registered and independently rated Good — all documentation available on request
  • 18 specialist care areas, each with dedicated trained staff
  • Established local teams in both Leeds and Cornwall
  • Trusted by local authorities, NHS commissioners, and social workers
  • Low staff turnover — carers stay because we genuinely support them

Getting Started

How to Arrange Care

Simple, straightforward steps from first contact to your care beginning — we guide families through every stage.

1

Free Initial Assessment

Contact us by phone or online form. We'll arrange a free, no-obligation assessment at a time that suits you — no pressure, just a conversation.

2

Personalised Care Plan

Our team creates a detailed, person-centred care plan around the individual's specific needs, preferences, and goals — reviewed regularly.

3

Care Begins

We match the right carers to each individual and begin providing consistent, reliable support — with a named key worker from day one.
